During this session, society members were divided into teams to collaboratively solve a series of Crypt Hunt-style challenges in preparation for the upcoming event during Cyber Safety Week. Each team tackled puzzles that required logical reasoning, careful observation, pattern recognition, and the application of fundamental cybersecurity concepts.

As teams progressed through the questions, members discussed multiple approaches, tested hypotheses, and combined their individual strengths to uncover solutions. The collaborative format encouraged effective communication, teamwork, and strategic thinking while exposing students to the types of challenges commonly encountered in cybersecurity competitions.

The session not only familiarised members with the structure and style of Crypt Hunt problems but also reinforced the importance of persistence, analytical thinking, and collaborative problem-solving when approaching complex technical challenges.
