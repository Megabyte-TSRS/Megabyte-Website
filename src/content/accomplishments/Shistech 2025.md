At Scottish High International School's Tech Quiz event, Quizzard, our team secured an impressive 1st place. The event had two stages -- a preliminary and a finals round. Teams had to face a series of challenging problems spanning across various topics, testing both depth of knowledge and quick thinking. 

The team consisted of:

1. Attharv Bhagchandka (grade 11)
2. Saiansh Tapuriah (grade 10)
3. Vedant Garg (grade 10)

At the Competetive Coding event, Codeclipse, Devyansh Garg (grade 11) and Ayaan Bansal (grade 10) both placed 2nd in the senior and middle category respectively. Participants had to solve a series of programming problems under a time limit, using any programming language they want. The winners were decided based on the accuracy of their code as well as the time they took to write it.
