The first session of Megabyte Society introduced members to the society's vision of exploring technology through innovation, collaboration, and hands-on learning. Students introduced themselves by sharing their names, grades, and one area of technology they were most interested in learning about, helping members discover shared interests and build connections within the society.

Members were then given an overview of the society's activities and opportunities throughout the year. This included **Cyber Safety Week**, featuring the annual **Crypt Hunt**, an interactive challenge designed to encourage logical thinking and problem-solving. Students were also introduced to **ShriTeq** and its events, the school's flagship tech fest, with team selections taking place after Cyber Safety Week.

Following the introduction, members participated in a practical vibe coding activity where they built simple websites using AI-powered development platforms such as **Lovable** and **Emergent**. Rather than focusing on traditional programming, students learned how natural language prompts could be used to generate, refine, and iterate on functional web applications. The activity demonstrated how modern AI tools are transforming software development by making rapid prototyping and experimentation more accessible, while also highlighting the importance of creativity, critical thinking, and effective prompt design.
