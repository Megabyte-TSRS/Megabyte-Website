This session explored the interdisciplinary applications of technology across multiple fields with society members selecting areas of interest to guide the discussion. The objective was to broaden members’ understanding of technology beyond conventional computing and highlight its role as a problem-solving tool across diverse domains.

Through interactive discussion and examples, society members examined how technological advancements influence efficiency, decision-making, and innovation in different fields. This helped establish a foundation for understanding how technology integrates with subject-specific challenges and real-world contexts.

The session then moved into a collaborative, activity-based component. Society members worked in groups to identify a real-world problem within their chosen field and design a practical, technology-driven solution. This activity encouraged critical thinking, creativity, teamwork, and the application of technological concepts to real life scenarios.
